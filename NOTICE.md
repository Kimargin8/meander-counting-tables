Kargin defined cluster cycles of meandric systems and studied the occurrences of individual shapes. Janson and Thévenin established a central limit theorem for the number of components of a given shape. The interval-component article studies avoidance of all cluster cycles and the total number of cluster cycles. [Kargin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.13449) · [Janson and Thévenin](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548324000117)
